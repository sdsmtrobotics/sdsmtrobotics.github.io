Super Robo Time


Information

Super Robo Time (SRT) is an annual event held by the Robotics Team, UAS, and 3D Print Club. It is a chance for people to learn about robotics, drones, and 3D printing, compete in a scavenger hunt in the labs, and construct a robot to complete a challenge. SRT generally spans over 3-4 weeks with the primary workdays on Saturdays and additional workdays and training sessions throughout.
