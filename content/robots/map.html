Namerobots.card[10].nameSwervebot
Tagrobots.card[10].tagASME
Categoryrobots.card[10].categoryasme
Yearsrobots.card[10].years11/29/2018 – 1/18/2019
Blurbrobots.card[10].blurbSwervebot is a drivetrain idea we had for the 2019 ASME Student Design Competition. Its main feature is a differential swerve drive system for omnidirectional motion. A standard swerve drive robot works by rotating wheels about their vertical axis so that they face in the direction the robot needs to move. This differential swerve drive design is more efficient since it does not require an additional motor for rotating the wheels (both motors are used for driving the robot).

Categoryrobots.card[12].categoryother
Yearsrobots.card[12].years10/1/2017 – 3/24/2018
Blurbrobots.card[12].blurbCustom controller developed for use with the soccorbots for the 2018 ASME SDC. Even though the controller was designed for this purpose, the primary goal was to create a controller that could easily be used with any of our robots in the future. Designing it ourselves would give us complete control of how the controller behaves and sends information to the robot. The controller used an ATMEGA328P as the controller and an XBee-Pro 900hp for communications.
