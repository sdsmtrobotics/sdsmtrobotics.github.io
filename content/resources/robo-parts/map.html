Introchips.introThe AVR chips the team programs with the Arduino toolchain, and the two boards it uses most. The bare chips are for when a project outgrows a development board and wants its own PCB.
Notechips.noteProgramming a bare ATTiny from the Arduino IDE needs the boards package: SpenceKonde/ATTinyCore.
