Eyebrowwhat.eyebrowThe event
Headingwhat.headingWhat Super Robo Time is
Paragraphwhat.paragraph
  1. Super Robo Time — SRT — is an annual event held by the Robotics Team, UAS and the 3D Print Club. It is a chance to learn about robotics, drones and 3D printing, compete in a scavenger hunt in the labs, and build a robot to complete a challenge.
  2. It generally runs over three to four weeks, with the main workdays on Saturdays and extra workdays and training sessions in between. You do not need to know anything going in, and you do not need to be on any of the three teams.
