Who we are

[who_we_are]

Section

Image altwho_we_are.image_altThe Rocker Robotics team on the steps of the Mines engineering building
Captionwho_we_are.captionThe team outside the engineering building on the Mines campus.
Eyebrowwho_we_are.eyebrowThe team
Headingwho_we_are.headingA student team, building from scratch
Paragraphwho_we_are.paragraph
  1. Every year we pick a competition, then design, build, and program a machine to enter it — mechanical, electrical, and software, all in house.
  2. Because our robots are built from the frame up, the work reaches into nearly every discipline on campus, and every major is welcome on the team. No experience is required to start: turn up to a meeting or a workday and we will find you something to build.
  3. We are part of the South Dakota Mines CAMP program, and we thank them for their support.

Eyebrowwhat_we_do.eyebrowHow a season runs
Headingwhat_we_do.headingDesign in the fall, compete in the spring
Paragraphwhat_we_do.paragraph
  1. Design starts during the fall semester and competition follows in the spring. In between are the workdays: machining and fabrication, wiring, CAD revisions, and long evenings spent getting the software to agree with the hardware.
  2. Alongside the competition robot there are seasonal builds — most visibly the trebuchet we run at the Great Downtown Pumpkin Festival on Main Street.
