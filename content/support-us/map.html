Why

[why]

Section

no image in this block
Eyebrowwhy.eyebrowWhere the money goes
Headingwhy.headingWhy we ask
Paragraphwhy.paragraph
  1. Building robots is amazing work. It is also an expensive way to spend a year. We are a non-profit student organization, so the team runs on school funding, fundraisers, and outside assistance — and without the last of those, a season can end before it reaches a competition.
  2. The competitions we enter are national. The robot that carries a company's logo is seen by students, faculty, and teams from across the country, and then by everyone who meets it at a festival, a school, or an event back home in Rapid City.
