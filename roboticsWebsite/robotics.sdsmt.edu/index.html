Welcome!

We are the South Dakota Mines Rocker Robotics Team, a multidisciplinary team from South Dakota School of Mines and Technology that focuses on building land competition vehicles. These are typically autonomous wheeled or tracked vehicles. Every year we choose a competition to participate in and spend the next two semesters designing, building, and programming a robot for competition.
